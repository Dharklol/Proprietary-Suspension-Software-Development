# Steering Level F Collection Readiness

## Ready now

- Versioned session/channel/calibration/raw/settled-point schemas.
- Exact-header and referential-integrity validator.
- Blank templates that do not invent missing instrumentation values.
- Rack-pot sensor identity linked to the shared Drive list.
- Planned primary-shaft measurement kept separate from the installed rack channel.
- Approach-specific centering and repeatability reduction utilities.

## Required before collection

- Perform and record `CAL-STEER-0001` for the rack potentiometer.
- Select, install, and calibrate the primary-shaft rotary potentiometer for `CAL-STEER-0002`.
- Declare logger device/input, sample rates, clocks, polarity, and zeros in the session channel file.
- Freeze the physical setup record and mounting photographs.

## Required before Level F acceptance

- At least three complete bidirectional sweeps.
- Installed left/right stop measurement.
- Rack-to-wheel and primary-shaft-to-rack response separation.
- Deadband, hysteresis, compliance, repeatability, and uncertainty reductions.
- An acceptance rule justified independently of the observed residuals.
