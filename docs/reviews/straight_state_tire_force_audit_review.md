# Straight-state tire force audit

## Question and conclusion

The native zero-curvature fixture has front lateral forces near +/-731 N.
This audit explains the numerical source of those forces without changing the
fixture, its tire model or vehicle equilibrium. It is conditional source-table
analysis and independent force projection, not physical correlation or setup
advice. All outputs remain zero-weight development evidence.

The large opposing front forces follow predominantly from the fixture's
roughly +/-1 degree front headings/slips, with substantial inclination response
and measured source bias. They are not generated by a nonzero cornering demand.
The frozen wheel reference explicitly specifies front static toe-out=-1 degree
(toe-in) and rear toe-out=+0.5 degree; positive toe-out is source-defined.
Small suspension/body/steering motions change those values in the solved state.
These are retained source-integration controls, not confirmed installed alignment
or the owner's intended setup. No per-wheel/total-toe reinterpretation is made.

## Four-query identity

For each wheel, transform to the actual measured-domain query (a,g), with g>=0.
For negative physical IA use a=-alpha, g=-gamma and multiply every force below
by s=-1; otherwise a=alpha, g=gamma and s=+1. Define the source-table force F.

The identity is:

```text
s F(a,g) = B + A + C + I
B = s F(0,0)
A = s [F(a,0)-F(0,0)]
C = s [F(0,g)-F(0,0)]
I = s [F(a,g)-F(a,0)-F(0,g)+F(0,0)]
```

This is an exact finite-difference decomposition at fixed Fz and pressure.
A is the slip increment at zero IA; C is the inclination increment at zero slip;
I retains their interaction. They are not independent additive tire force laws
or a unique causal attribution of a nonlinear surface.

For negative IA, the zero-IA entries above are the original reflected-side
limits, not the direct physical gamma=0 rule. The report separately evaluates
direct gamma=0 at the physical alpha and reports the boundary difference. This
distinction prevents measured source bias from being hidden in a supposed camber
effect. No seam is crossed by vehicle continuation and no bias is subtracted.

## Independent straight-state balance

For each road heading delta, project local Fy explicitly as
(-Fy sin(delta), Fy cos(delta), 0). All four projected longitudinal components
are negative, despite near cancellation of lateral components. Their sum is
approximately (-29.08032541, -0.00051398, 0) N.

The solved virtual holder supplies approximately
(29.08032541, +0.00051398, 0) N and 232.6426 W at V=8 m/s.
The independent force-plus-holder residual is below 8e-9 N; sum(Fz)-m*g is about
9e-13 N. The established 1e-6 N force criterion is retained. The full independent
source equilibrium wrench is carried alongside this scalar/global check.

Thus zero curvature does not require a zero holder when the fixture has toe and
signed tire forces. This audit does not make its 232.6 W a physical drivetrain,
rolling-resistance or measured tire-dissipation prediction; the pure-lateral
model and virtual-holder assumptions still apply.

## Verification and next boundary

Three tests cover an independently manufactured nonadditive surface, reflected
one-sided versus direct zero-IA separation with unchanged load/pressure, and all
four wheel decompositions plus independent horizontal/vertical balance. Source
query failures propagate rather than producing a repaired diagnostic.

The force magnitudes are explained within the current source/geometry chain;
physical adequacy is not established by that explanation. Next, verify the
manufactured zero-alignment/low-force limit and map the supported computational
envelope. Any native alignment perturbation needs a distinct configuration and
vehicle re-equilibration. No baseline force is reused to rank a changed geometry.
