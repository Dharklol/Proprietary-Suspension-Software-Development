## Frozen numerical results

The WUFR-27 baseline completes the fifteen-state historical target sweep with a wheel-heading regression error of **0.625925977° RMS**. The left and right RMS values are equal under the frozen exact-reflection model, and the maximum absolute residual is **1.36193623°**. The nonzero error is expected because the target is a historical polynomial fit and does not exactly reproduce the current analyzer.

The synthetic recovery fixture uses a source rack longitudinal offset of **0.020 m**. The best candidate returns **0.0199951171875 m**, an absolute recovery error of **0.0000048828125 m**, and a response residual of **0.000242432337° RMS**. Both are comfortably inside the frozen tolerances of 0.001 m and 0.001° RMS. The search evaluates 75 candidates, of which 73 are feasible and two are infeasible, and retains eight candidates for comparison.

These values are frozen in `steering_nominal_optimizer_result_v0.1.0.toml`. They establish software behavior and historical regression consistency only.

## Review conclusions

The implementation closes the first nominal inverse-design search layer. It demonstrates that the role-selectable geometry generator and authoritative analyzer can support deterministic target recovery and candidate comparison without introducing a second steering model or hiding infeasible mechanisms inside a penalty score.

The current ranking is intentionally narrow. It contains one active response objective and only the first-layer mechanism constraints. The lowest target error cannot yet be interpreted as the best physical steering design because steering-arm material, rack and chassis packaging, wheel and brake clearance, articulation, thread engagement, stops, manufacturing limits, sensitivity, tire behavior, and effort are not represented.

The broad development bounds may produce concepts that cannot be manufactured or installed. The report correctly preserves this limitation. The tool may be used for exploratory nominal geometry studies after review and merge, but not for WUFR-27 redesign, WUFR-28 release, automatic CAD modification, or physical-performance claims.

Keep robustness, discrete hardware, suspension-pose, tire-target, effort, and physical-parameter work behind their existing gates.
